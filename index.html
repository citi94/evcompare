What changes in 2028?

  • EVs: Electric Vehicle Excise Duty (eVED) of 3p/mile from 1 April 2028, on top of annual VED
  • Fuel: duty is 52.95p/litre until 31 Dec 2026, then 57.95p from March 2027 once the 5p cut is gone. Add about 3.5p of estimated RPI rises by 2028, plus VAT: about +10.2p/litre at the pump

Sources: GOV.UK eVED policy paper (Jul 2026); Amended Fuel Duty rates 2026 to 2027 (May 2026). The RPI rises are an estimate. Pump prices are otherwise held at today's level.
